Fifteen labs,
six sets of notes.

The Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate, completed March 2026. The labs that produced a real artefact — a risk register, a PASTA threat model, an incident handler's journal — are written up here with the artefact itself.

Governance and risk

4 labs

  • Security AuditsNIST CSF, compliance against PCI DSS, GDPR and SOC, worked through the Botium Toys case study.→
  • Risk ManagementBuilding a risk register, an asset inventory and a scoring matrix.→
  • Vulnerability AssessmentNIST SP 800-30, risk scoring and remediation planning.→
  • Threat Modeling — PASTAAll seven PASTA stages, with attack trees and data flow diagrams.→

Network

4 labs

  • Network Traffic AnalysisDNS, ICMP and UDP analysis, and reading the logs they produce.→
  • Wireshark LabsPacket analysis, display filters, TCP and HTTP inspection.→
  • tcpdump LabsCapturing traffic at the command line and interpreting the output.→
  • Network HardeningMFA, firewalls, patch management and port filtering.→

Threats and response

3 labs

  • Incident ResponseThe NIST IR lifecycle, incident handler journals, ransomware and denial of service.→
  • Brute Force AttacksAttack types, the log evidence each leaves, and prevention.→
  • Social EngineeringUSB baiting, phishing and physical security.→

Foundations

4 labs

  • Linux File Permissionschmod, permission strings and least privilege in practice.→
  • SQL SecuritySecurity-focused queries and investigating a login log.→
  • CryptographyHashing, SHA-256, the Caesar cipher and OpenSSL.→
  • Access ControlAuthentication, authorisation and least-privilege models.→

The deeper defensive work is in the HTB Academy modules and the investigations.
