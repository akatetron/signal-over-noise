06 / Scope

What this work is — and isn't

I'd rather state this plainly than have you wonder.

// what it is

Detection logic and investigation write-ups I produced myself against HTB Academy and Google lab environments. The reasoning, tuning notes and escalation criteria are my own.

// what it isn't

Production incident response. I haven't yet held a SOC role, so none of this ran against live enterprise telemetry. The rules are documented starting points, not battle-tested content.

// why it matters

Tier 1 hiring is about judgement — knowing why SubStatus 0xC0000072 outranks an ordinary failed logon, or why a finding goes to IT Ops instead of Tier 2. That transfers.
